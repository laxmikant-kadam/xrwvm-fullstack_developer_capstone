About Cars Dealership

Three decades of putting drivers first

We are a family owned car retailer with twelve branches across six states. Our promise is simple: fair prices, certified vehicles and a buying experience you would happily recommend to a friend.

Our story

From a single lot in Wichita to a national retailer

Cars Dealership opened its doors in 1994 with eight used cars, a folding table and one rule: never sell a vehicle you would not drive yourself. That rule still guides every branch we open.

Today our teams deliver more than twelve thousand vehicles a year, supported by certified technicians, in-house finance specialists and a customer care centre that answers the phone on the second ring. We invest in digital tools such as online inventory browsing and sentiment-analysed reviews so that customers can make decisions with complete confidence.

  • 172-point certification on every vehicle we sell
  • No-haggle pricing published online before you visit
  • 7-day exchange promise on every purchase
